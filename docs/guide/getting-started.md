# Get started

## Open a folder

1. Press `⌘O`.
2. Choose a folder. You can also drop a folder on the window.

The Grid shows your photos at once. There is no import step.

Oxys opens these file types:

- RAW: ARW, CR2, CR3, NEF, RAF, DNG, ORF, RW2, PEF.
- Other: JPEG, HEIC, TIFF.

Oxys opens one folder. It does not look in subfolders. A camera card keeps photos in a folder such as `DCIM/100CANON`. Open that folder. If the folder has no photos but a subfolder does, Oxys tells you.

## Make your first choices

1. Press `Return` on a photo in the Grid. The photo opens in Loupe. Loupe shows one photo.
2. Press `→` for the next photo. Press `←` for the previous photo. Hold the key to move fast.
3. Press `1` to `5` for stars. Press `0` to clear the stars.
4. Press `X` to reject a photo.
5. Press `6` for red, `7` for yellow, `8` for green, or `9` for blue.
6. Hold `⇧` and press one of these keys. Oxys applies your choice and goes to the next photo.
7. Press `Esc` to go back to the Grid.

A small badge shows each choice at once. Oxys saves the choice to disk in the background. You do not wait. If you quit, you lose nothing.

## Show only your best photos

Press `⌥⌘3` to show photos with 3 stars or more. Press `⌘A` to select them. Press `⌘R` to show them in Finder. See [Filter, sort, and send photos on](filtering.md).

## Where Oxys saves your choices

Oxys writes each choice to a small `.xmp` file next to the photo. This file is a sidecar. Lightroom Classic, RawTherapee, and ART can read it. Oxys never changes your RAW file.

Before you open the folder in another program, read [Sidecars and other apps](sidecars.md).

## The window

- **Toolbar:** a Grid, Loupe, and Compare picker. It also has buttons for the filter bar and the inspector.
- **Title:** the folder name. The subtitle shows how many photos you see, for example "312 of 1,204 shown".
- **`⇥` (Tab):** hides the toolbar and panels. Press it again to show them. Moving the pointer does not show them.
- **Full screen:** press `⌃⌘F`.
