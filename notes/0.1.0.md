First public preview of Oxys, a keyboard-first RAW culler for macOS. It is a preview: it works, but some parts have not been tested on real setups yet (see "Known limits").

### What you can do

- **Cull:** rate with stars, set color labels, reject. Undo and redo work. Each decision is saved as an XMP sidecar next to the photo. Oxys never writes to your original files, and it picks up sidecar changes made by other apps.
- **View:** Loupe and Grid. Fit, 1:1 and zoom steps, panning, and zoom that carries over to the next photo. RAW files are developed on demand.
- **Know what you are looking at:** a badge shows whether the picture is the embedded preview or the developed RAW.
- **Check the shot:** focus peaking, highlight and shadow clipping, and a histogram.
- **Compare:** two photos side by side with linked zoom. Values that differ in the EXIF are marked.
- **Read the data:** EXIF with lens and AF point, an inspector, and an info strip over the photo.
- **Find photos:** filter and sort bar, selection of many photos, Reveal in Finder.
- **Hand off:** open photos in an external editor, extract the embedded JPEGs, or export developed JPEG and HEIC (`⇧⌘E`).
- **RAW+JPEG pairs** are kept together. **Auto-advance** moves to the next photo after a decision. **Session resume** reopens your folder where you left it.
- Everything is reachable from the keyboard. The cheat sheet and the menu bar list every key.

### Known limits

- Only ART has been checked for sidecar compatibility. Lightroom Classic and RawTherapee are not tested. Lightroom Classic may ignore sidecars for JPEG, DNG and TIFF.
- You cannot change the keys yet.
- A cold photo can load slower than the speed targets, and memory use can go above 2 GB on very large folders.
- VoiceOver and Full Keyboard Access have not been tested.
- Needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 27 or newer.
